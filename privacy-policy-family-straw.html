Privacy Policy for Family Straw

Last Updated: March 18, 2026

Nailogic ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Family Straw mobile application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our app and the choices you have associated with that data.

Information Collection and Use

We built Family Straw with privacy in mind.

  • Personal Data: We do not collect personally identifiable information (such as your name, address, or email) unless you voluntarily provide it (e.g., for support).
  • Sensitive Permissions: If the app requests permissions (such as camera or microphone), they are used strictly for in-app functionality and are not stored or transmitted to our servers without your explicit consent.
  • Automated Data: We may collect non-identifying information such as device type, operating system version, and basic usage statistics to improve app performance and fix bugs.

Children's Privacy (COPPA/Families Policy Compliance)

Family Straw is designed for families and children.

We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

We do not use interest-based advertising or remarketing.

Any third-party SDKs or APIs used in the app are certified for use in child-directed services and comply with Google Play's Families Policy.

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your data to third parties. We only share information:

  • To comply with legal obligations.
  • To protect and defend our rights or property.
  • With service providers who assist in app analytics, provided they adhere to strict confidentiality.

Data Security, Retention, and Deletion

The security of your data is important to us. We use industry-standard encryption to protect data in transit. We only retain data for as long as necessary to provide app functionality.

Request Deletion: Users can request the deletion of any data we may have by contacting us at the email below.
